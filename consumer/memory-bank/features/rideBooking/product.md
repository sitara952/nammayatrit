# Ride Booking Product Context

## Problem Statement

*   Users need an efficient and reliable way to book transportation services.
*   The application should facilitate the entire booking lifecycle, from searching for options to managing ongoing and completed rides.

## Target Audience

*   End-users requiring transportation services.

## Proposed Solution Features

*   **Service/Ride Booking & Management:**
    *   Searching for available rides/services (e.g., based on origin, destination, service type).
    *   Viewing ride options, details (e.g., price, ETA, vehicle type), and selecting a preferred option.
    *   Confirming pickup and drop-off locations.
    *   Making a booking.
    *   Viewing and managing current/upcoming bookings (MyRides, MyBookingDetails).
    *   Viewing past booking history.
    *   Cancelling bookings (with applicable policies).
*   **Real-time Tracking/Updates:**
    *   Tracking the location of an assigned vehicle in real-time (FollowRide).
    *   Receiving updates on ride status (e.g., driver assigned, driver arriving, ride started, ride completed).
*   **Location Services & Maps:**
    *   Utilizing maps for displaying routes, vehicle locations, and selecting pickup/drop-off points.
    *   Searching for addresses and points of interest.

## User Experience (UX) Goals

*   **Efficient Search & Booking:** Quick and easy to find and book a suitable ride.
*   **Clear Information:** Transparent pricing, ETAs, and ride details.
*   **Reliable Tracking:** Accurate and timely updates on ride progress.
*   **Intuitive Map Interaction:** Easy to use maps for location selection and tracking.

## Success Metrics

*   *Number of successful bookings.*
*   *Time taken to complete a booking.*
*   *Ride cancellation rates (and reasons).*
*   *User satisfaction with search results and ride options.*
*   *Accuracy of ETAs and real-time tracking.*
